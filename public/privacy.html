Privacy

Plain Bible, on the web and in the iOS app, has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

On your device

Your settings, highlights, bookmarks and recent searches are stored on your device only. Searching happens on your device too.

Optional sync

If you choose to sign in, the app stores one document for your account on our sync service (hosted by Cloudflare): your highlights, bookmarks and settings, so they match on all your devices. Sign-in uses a passkey: we keep its public key, never a password, email address or name. You can delete your account and everything stored with it at any time from Settings → Account and sync.

Downloads

The app downloads Bible versions from this website. Like any website, the hosting service (GitHub Pages) briefly sees your IP address when you download a file. We don't log or keep it.
