A search that finds things

A word, a phrase, two words near each other, in one Bible or many, with the results counted by book. Type a reference and you're there. Strong's numbers, too.

Works offline

The Bibles you read stay on your phone. On a plane, in the hills, in a basement: it opens on the verse where you left off, with no connection at all.

Highlights, notes and bookmarks

Eight colors, a note on a verse or on a whole passage, a ribbon for the places you come back to. Bring over what you kept in another Bible app.

🙏

Prayer

Record a prayer with the 🙏 button, or pray using a verse. Recordings stay on your device, or sync locked with a key that only you hold.

Circles

A small private circle for family and friends. Share a verse, a note or a prayer, and reply with a word or a 🙏. What you share is locked with the circle's own key, and opens in each reader's own Bible.

Your devices, together

Only if you want it. Sign in with a passkey or a code sent by email, and your highlights, notes and settings follow you. A second device signs in from the first with one link; nothing to type.

Put it on your Home Screen

Then it opens full screen like any other app, keeps your place, and works with no connection.

  1. Open the Bible in Safari.
  2. Tap the menu button in the address bar, then Share . On older iPhones, Share is right in the bar at the bottom.
  3. Tap View More, then Add to Home Screen .
  4. Leave Open as Web App on and tap Add. The lamp appears on your Home Screen.

Do it from the Bible, not from this page, so the lamp opens straight into your reading. Use Safari: other browsers on iPhone and iPad can't always add an app.

Apple’s own instructions ↗

One tap. Nothing goes through an app store.

Installed. Open the lamp with your other apps.

  1. Open the Bible in Chrome, or the browser you use.
  2. Open the browser's menu, usually three dots at the top right.
  3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen, then Install.
  4. The lamp appears with your other apps.

Google’s own instructions ↗

It opens in its own window, like any other app.

Installed. Open My Quiet Bible with your other apps.

  1. Open the Bible in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, a screen with an arrow , then Install.
  3. On a Mac with Safari: File, then Add to Dock.
  4. Or simply keep the tab. It works the same, and remembers your place.
